Help & frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common concerns.

Is Digital Learn 2.0 an official government website?

No. It is an independent educational learning project and is not affiliated with UIDAI, DigiLocker, the Income Tax Department or any government agency.

Will this website apply for Aadhaar or PAN on my behalf?

No. It explains common steps. Applications, updates and downloads must be completed through the appropriate official portal or authorised channel.

Why should I never share an OTP?

An OTP can authorise access or a transaction. Never share it with callers, agents in unsolicited messages or anyone who says they need it to help you.

What data can be saved in the Google Sheet?

With the integration configured, the project can record your name, age group, education level, quiz score and module completion status. It should not collect Aadhaar/PAN numbers, OTPs or document files.

Will my progress follow me to another device?

This starter keeps the current profile and progress in this browser and sends activity events to the Sheet when configured. Cross-device progress requires a properly designed sign-in and backend.
